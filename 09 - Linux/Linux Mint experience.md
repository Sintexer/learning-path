After trying [[Ubuntu experience|Ubuntu]] I switched to Linux Mint. I heard of it as a stable, convenient Linux system, which is Ubuntu-like, but has another desktop environment. Installation process was like a breeze, everything went cool. I've installed the system and was pretty happy with it. After Ubuntu, I didn't quite enjoy Cinnamon, but I respected the long list of pre-installed apps on Mint. I hate it now, though. After a couple of hours, the system began to lag enormously. It just lagged for several seconds straight occasionally. Like I am scrolling the forum - bam - lag for 6 seconds. I am trying to open task manager - bam - another lag. I tried to google the problem, but I just haven't had power to deal with this problem. One can say I just gave up - after three days of f with Linux I just wanted a working system back, despite the distribution. So I looked to [[Fedora experience|Fedora]] - the only Linux I was happy about before.
