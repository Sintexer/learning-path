> AKA **Network layer**

This [[OSI Model]] layer is responsible for **logical addressing and routing** data packets across interconnected networks (from source host to destination host across multiple hops).

- **PDU**: Packet
- **Examples**: IPv4, IPv6, ICMP (`ping`/`traceroute`), BGP, OSPF, IPsec ([[VPN]]), routers, [[CIDR Notation]], [[Subnet]], [[Virtual Private Cloud|VPC]] route tables.
