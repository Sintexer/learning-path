> AKA **International Organization for Standardization**

**ISO** is an independent, non-governmental international body that publishes technical, industrial, and commercial standards used worldwide.

In software engineering and networking, ISO is most commonly referenced for:
- **[[OSI Model]] (ISO/IEC 7498)**: the 7-layer conceptual model for network communication.
- **ISO 8601**: unambiguous date/time format (`2026-11-22T14:00:00Z`), critical for logs, APIs, and [[Distributed Tracing]].
- **ISO/IEC 27001**: information security management standard (ISMS).
- **ISO/IEC 9075**: the [[SQL]] language standard.
